Layout engine demo

Development-only page (not part of the app build). Each panel is one plan for a canvas ratio and dial value; the border is clamped to maxBorderForAspect(plan, w, h, 3). portrait slot landscape slot roughly square

In the photo-fit views each slot holds a photo of known aspect (see “Photo mix”). The light rectangle is the whole photo shrunk to fit inside the slot; the dark margin around it is the share cropped away when the photo fills the slot. Colour runs from green (no crop) to red (half or more cropped). “Crop” is the area-weighted mean crop fraction; auto is assignPhotos, random a seeded shuffle.
